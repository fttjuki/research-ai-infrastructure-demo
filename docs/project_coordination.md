# Fictional experiment coordination plan

This plan is a portfolio example for coordinating a small online study. Roles and timing are illustrative; no real people or partner organizations are involved.

| Phase | Timing | Owner | Deliverable / check | Main risk and response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design and preregistration | Week 1 | Research lead + lab manager | Approved questions, outcomes, exclusions, and analysis plan | Design changes after seeing results; freeze the plan before data collection |
| Build and test survey | Week 2 | Lab manager + survey researcher | Consent, eligibility, 3 randomized arms, attention check, completion route | Broken links or missing fields; test every path with synthetic test accounts |
| Pilot | Week 3 | Lab manager + project partner | 30-person pilot plan, timing report, issue log | Low completion or confusing wording; pause launch and revise before main recruitment |
| Main recruitment | Weeks 4–5 | Research lead + recruitment contact | Recruitment status, response count, participant-payment reconciliation | Slow or uneven recruitment; agree a documented adjustment before changing criteria |
| Data quality and handover | Week 6 | Data analyst + lab manager | Frozen raw export, checksum, codebook, cleaning report | Identifier leakage; store linkage separately and restrict access |
| Analysis and communication | Weeks 7–8 | Analyst + research lead | Reproducible analysis, results summary, seminar/partner update | Overstating synthetic or uncertain results; label limitations and have a human review |

## Coordination checklist

- Name one decision owner for protocol changes and one operational contact for the survey platform.
- Record partner approvals, launch dates, changes, incidents, and handover decisions in the project log.
- Do not change recruitment criteria or exclusions informally after launch; document and approve changes.
- Confirm consent language, data location, retention period, and payment workflow with the institution before any real study.
- Give collaborators a short status update at each phase gate: completed, next action, owner, blocker.
